Where We Work

Based in Gulu City. Working across Northern Uganda and beyond.

Omalo Graphics Centre Ltd is based in Koch Goma, Gulu City, and serves clients across Northern Uganda from that base. Government offices, NGOs, schools, churches and businesses throughout the region bring their design, branding and print work to our studio.

We also work with clients outside the region, who send artwork and instructions remotely and receive finished work through an agreed arrangement. Distance does not change the standard we hold ourselves to.

Omalo Graphics Centre Ltd is a modern and forward looking graphics, printing, branding and visual communication company built around quality, technology, creativity and professionalism. With five years of experience, advanced equipment and a dedicated team, we are ready to transform your ideas into professional, high quality and impactful visual communication.
